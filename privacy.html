BuyersAnswered — Privacy Policy

Run by: LeadGendary Services LLC

Website: buyersanswered.openedgecreator.com

Contact: support@openedgecreator.com

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what data BuyersAnswered collects, why it collects it, and what you can do about it. If anything on this page is not clear, contact us at support@openedgecreator.com.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers TikTok Shop sellers who connect a TikTok account to BuyersAnswered (called "sellers" on this page), and visitors to buyersanswered.openedgecreator.com.

2. What data we access, and why

We only access data for one purpose: to find buying questions in the comments under a seller's own TikTok videos, and to draft replies to those questions for the seller to approve.

From TikTok, for a connected seller account only:

  • Comments on the seller's own videos — the text of comments and replies, the name of the person who wrote the comment, and the time the comment was posted. We use this to find buying questions (for example, questions about price, colour, size, shipping, or stock) and to draft replies.
  • Basic information about the seller's own videos — such as the video title and which product a video is linked to. We use this to match a comment to the right product.
  • The seller's product catalog — product names, prices, colours, sizes, and stock information for the seller's TikTok Shop products. We use this so a drafted reply gives the correct price, shade name, size, or stock answer instead of a guessed answer.

    • From the seller directly:

  • Account information — name and email address used to create a BuyersAnswered account.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Approval actions — a record of which drafted replies the seller approved, edited, or skipped, and when. We use this for the seller's daily report and to keep an accurate record of what was posted.

    • From website visitors:

  • Basic technical information — such as browser type and pages visited on buyersanswered.openedgecreator.com, used to keep the website working and secure.

    • 3. Permission comes first

    We access TikTok data only for accounts that have connected to BuyersAnswered and granted permission through TikTok's own authorisation process.

  • We request only two TikTok permissions: permission to read comments on the seller's own videos, and permission to post a reply after the seller approves it.
  • We do not access TikTok accounts that have not connected and granted permission.
  • We never ask for, see, or store a seller's TikTok password.
  • A seller can disconnect the TikTok account at any time, from inside BuyersAnswered or from TikTok's own settings. When an account is disconnected, we stop accessing its TikTok data.

    • 4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not rent personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties for their advertising.
  • We do not post any reply to a comment without the seller's approval.
  • We do not use a seller's comments, catalog, or customer information to advertise to that seller's customers for our own benefit.

    • 5. Who else processes data for us

    We use a small number of service providers to run BuyersAnswered, such as hosting, payment processing, and email delivery. These providers process data only so that BuyersAnswered works, and only under contract. We share with each provider only the data it needs to do its job.

    6. How long we keep data

  • Comments and drafted replies: kept while the seller's account is active, so the seller can see their daily reports and reply history. Comments and draft history are deleted 90 days after they are created, or sooner if the seller asks.
  • Product catalog information: kept while the account is active, and refreshed from the seller's catalog. Deleted when the account is closed, subject to the deletion timing in section 7.
  • Account and billing records: kept for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and legal records.

    • 7. Deletion

  • When a seller disconnects their TikTok account, we stop all new access to that account's TikTok data right away.
  • When a seller closes their BuyersAnswered account, or writes to support@openedgecreator.com and asks us to delete their data, we delete the seller's stored comments, drafts, and catalog copy within 30 days, except for billing and legal records we must keep by law.
  • A seller can ask at any time what data we hold about them, and can ask for a copy or for corrections, by writing to support@openedgecreator.com.

    • 8. Security

    We protect stored data with access controls and encryption in transit. Only team members who need data to run or support the service can access it. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach affects a seller's data, we will notify affected sellers as required by law.

    9. Children

    BuyersAnswered is a tool for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

    10. Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy in a way that matters — for example, if we ever ask for a new kind of data — we will update this page, change the date at the top, and notify active sellers by email before the change takes effect.
