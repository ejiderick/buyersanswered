Who this tool is for

This tool is for TikTok Shop sellers in the United States who:

  • Sell beauty or personal care products
  • Post videos of their products regularly
  • Get more comments than they can answer quickly by hand
  • Have missed sales because a buying question was answered late, or not answered at all

When you do not need it

If you only get a few comments a week, you do not need this tool. If you get buying questions every day and some of them wait hours for an answer, this tool is built for you.

What counts as a buying question?

Questions about price, colour, size, shipping, and whether an item is in stock. Those are the comments BuyersAnswered picks out first.

How it works, in 3 steps

Nothing is posted without your approval. That rule runs through every step.

1

Connect your TikTok account

You sign in with your TikTok account and give BuyersAnswered permission to read comments on your own videos and to post replies you approve. The tool cannot see or touch any account that has not connected and given this permission. You can disconnect at any time.

2

The tool watches comments and drafts replies

The tool reads new comments under your videos. It picks out the buying questions — price, colour, size, shipping, and stock. For each one, it drafts a reply using your real product list, so prices, shade names, sizes, and stock answers are correct. Nothing is posted yet.

3

You approve, and you get a daily report

Each draft waits for you. You tap Approve to post it, edit it first, or skip it. Nothing is posted without your approval. At the end of each day, you get a short report: how many buying comments came in, how many you answered, and any that are still waiting.

Questions and answers

Plain answers to the questions sellers ask first.

Do I need to give you my TikTok password?

No. You connect your account through TikTok's own sign-in page. BuyersAnswered never sees or stores your TikTok password.

What permission does the tool ask for?

Two permissions only: (1) permission to read comments on your own videos, and (2) permission to post a reply to a comment, only after you approve that reply. The tool does not ask for permission to post videos, change your account, or read your private messages.

Can the tool read comments on other people's videos?

No. The tool only reads comments on videos from the TikTok account you connect. It cannot access any account that has not connected and given permission.

Does the tool post replies by itself?

No. Every reply is a draft first. A reply is posted only after you tap Approve. If you do nothing, nothing is posted.

Where do the answers in the replies come from?

From your own product list: your product names, prices, colours, sizes, and stock information. The tool does not guess a price or a shade name. If the tool cannot find the answer in your product list, it flags the comment for you instead of drafting a reply with a guessed answer.

What is the daily report?

One short report at the end of each day. It lists the buying comments the tool found that day, which ones you answered, and which ones are still waiting for you. Its purpose is simple: you can see, in one place, that no buying question was missed.

What happens if I disconnect my account or cancel?

The tool stops reading your comments and stops drafting replies right away when you disconnect. When you cancel your subscription, you are not charged again. You can also ask us to delete your stored data — see our Privacy page for how.

This page explains, in plain words, what data BuyersAnswered collects, why it collects it, and what you can do about it. If anything on this page is not clear, contact us at support@openedgecreator.com.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers TikTok Shop sellers who connect a TikTok account to BuyersAnswered (called “sellers” on this page), and visitors to buyersanswered.openedgecreator.com.

2. What data we access, and why

We only access data for one purpose: to find buying questions in the comments under a seller's own TikTok videos, and to draft replies to those questions for the seller to approve.

From TikTok, for a connected seller account only:

  • Comments on the seller's own videos — the text of comments and replies, the name of the person who wrote the comment, and the time the comment was posted. We use this to find buying questions and to draft replies.
  • Basic information about the seller's own videos — such as the video title and which product a video is linked to. We use this to match a comment to the right product.
  • The seller's product catalog — product names, prices, colours, sizes, and stock information. We use this so a drafted reply gives the correct answer instead of a guessed answer.

From the seller directly:

  • Account information — name and email address used to create a BuyersAnswered account.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Approval actions — a record of which drafted replies the seller approved, edited, or skipped, and when.

From website visitors: basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the website working and secure.

3. Permission comes first

We access TikTok data only for accounts that have connected to BuyersAnswered and granted permission through TikTok's own authorisation process. We request only two TikTok permissions: permission to read comments on the seller's own videos, and permission to post a reply after the seller approves it. We do not access TikTok accounts that have not connected and granted permission. We never ask for, see, or store a seller's TikTok password. A seller can disconnect at any time, from inside BuyersAnswered or from TikTok's own settings. When an account is disconnected, we stop accessing its TikTok data.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not rent personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties for their advertising.
  • We do not post any reply to a comment without the seller's approval.
  • We do not use a seller's comments, catalog, or customer information to advertise to that seller's customers for our own benefit.

5. Who else processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers to run BuyersAnswered, such as hosting, payment processing, and email delivery. These providers process data only so that BuyersAnswered works, and only under contract. We share with each provider only the data it needs to do its job.

6. How long we keep data

  • Comments and drafted replies: kept while the seller's account is active, so the seller can see their daily reports and reply history. They are deleted 90 days after they are created, or sooner if the seller asks.
  • Product catalog information: kept while the account is active, and refreshed from the seller's catalog. Deleted when the account is closed, subject to section 7.
  • Account and billing records: kept for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and legal records.

7. Deletion

When a seller disconnects their TikTok account, we stop all new access to that account's TikTok data right away. When a seller closes their BuyersAnswered account, or writes to support@openedgecreator.com and asks us to delete their data, we delete the seller's stored comments, drafts, and catalog copy within 30 days, except for billing and legal records we must keep by law. A seller can ask at any time what data we hold about them, and can ask for a copy or for corrections, by writing to support@openedgecreator.com.

8. Security

We protect stored data with access controls and encryption in transit. Only team members who need data to run or support the service can access it. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach affects a seller's data, we will notify affected sellers as required by law.

9. Children

BuyersAnswered is a tool for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters — for example, if we ever ask for a new kind of data — we will update this page, change the date at the top, and notify active sellers by email before the change takes effect.
